# LikeHome product decisions

- Cancellation more than 48 hours before check-in has no charge; within 48 hours is 20% of the reservation total; after check-in is not allowed.
- One point is earned per dollar spent, only after a stay is completed.
- 1,000 points redeem one free night, capped at the selected room's nightly price.
- Preview uses Demo Payment Mode and sample data when Supabase or Stripe variables are not configured; no real card data is accepted or stored.
