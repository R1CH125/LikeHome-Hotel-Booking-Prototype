# LikeHome demo script

1. Start on Home and search “California” for two guests.
2. Open Sundial House, compare rooms, and choose dates at least three days out.
3. Continue to Checkout and point out the nightly price, nights, subtotal, cancellation policy, and visible Demo Payment Mode banner.
4. Sign up inline when prompted and confirm the reservation.
5. Open My Bookings to show the confirmation number and paid status.
6. Change the reservation dates and show the recalculated total.
7. Return to My Bookings, open cancellation, show the policy, and confirm cancellation.
8. Open Rewards to show the points explanation and ledger.
