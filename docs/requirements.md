# LikeHome requirements

LikeHome is a semester-project hotel booking application. Visitors can discover sample stays, search and filter rooms, choose dates and guests, and view details. Authenticated users can create, change, and cancel reservations, see only their own bookings, pay in a safe demo mode, and participate in a points program.

The server owns identity, availability, overlap prevention, pricing, reward balance, cancellation rules, and payment status. The demonstration cancellation rules are no charge more than 48 hours before check-in, 20% within 48 hours, and no website cancellation after check-in. Users earn one point per dollar on completed stays, and 1,000 points redeem one free night up to the selected room's nightly rate.
